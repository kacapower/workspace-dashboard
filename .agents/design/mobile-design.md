# Mobile Design Guidelines
## Navigation
- Fixed bottom tab bar (Height: 50px-60px).
- Icons: Dashboard, Monitored Profiles, Alerts, Settings.
## Header
- Sticky top bar with App Name/Logo on left, context actions on right.
## Content
- Single column layout.
- Use horizontal scroll (overflow-x: auto, scroll-snap) for quick profile switching (like a Story tray).
## Interactions
- Minimum touch target: 44x44px.
- Swipe to dismiss alerts or delete monitored profiles.
## Breakpoints tested
- 320px, 375px, 430px.
