# Instagram Responsive Research
## What was researched
How Instagram scales from mobile to tablet to desktop.
## Source
Instagram Web.
## Date accessed
August 27, 2026
## Relevant observations
- Mobile: Bottom nav, single column.
- Tablet: Sidebar (icon only), single column feed or wider grid.
- Desktop: Wide sidebar (icons + text), feed centered, additional suggestions on the right.
## UI pattern
- Expanding sidebars and centered max-width content areas.
## UX reasoning
- Maximizes screen real estate on desktop while keeping content readable.
## How the pattern could apply to Insta Fixer
- The dashboard should adopt the left-sidebar approach on desktop to leave room for large data tables and analytics on the right.
## What should NOT be copied
- The right-side "Suggestions for you" pane (unnecessary for a utility app).
## Implementation implications
- Breakpoints at 768px and 1024px.
