# Instagram Feed UX Research
## What was researched
Home feed layout, post cards.
## Source
Mobbin, UXArchive.
## Date accessed
August 27, 2026
## Relevant observations
- Header: App logo, notifications, messages.
- Story tray at the very top.
- Post cards: User header (avatar + name + more options), Media, Action bar (Like, Comment, Share, Save), Likes count, Caption (truncated with "more"), timestamp.
## UI pattern
- Vertical list of cards with high contrast between media and background.
## UX reasoning
- Focuses attention on one piece of content at a time while allowing quick interactions.
## How the pattern could apply to Insta Fixer
- A timeline of changes (e.g., "User changed bio") can be displayed like a feed, showing the "Before" and "After" as the media.
## What should NOT be copied
- The "Like" button, as it's not a social app.
## Implementation implications
- Efficient rendering of lists if monitoring history gets long.
