# Instagram Story UX Research
## What was researched
Ephemeral content consumption patterns.
## Source
Meta Design guidelines.
## Date accessed
August 27, 2026
## Relevant observations
- Horizontal scrolling tray at the top of the feed.
- Gradient border for unseen, gray for seen.
- Full-screen vertical viewing with progress bars at the top.
## UI pattern
- Circular avatars in the tray.
## UX reasoning
- FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out) and low friction for sharing.
## How the pattern could apply to Insta Fixer
- Use the horizontal circular avatar tray to show "Recently Updated" monitored profiles.
## What should NOT be copied
- The auto-advancing full-screen story view.
## Implementation implications
- Overflow-x: auto with scroll-snap for the tray.
